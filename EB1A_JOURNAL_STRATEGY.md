## EB1A REQUIREMENTS

To qualify for EB1A, you need evidence of **extraordinary ability** in the sciences, arts, or business. Publications matter, but the **quality and impact** of journals matter more than quantity.

### Key EB1A Criteria (need 3 of 10):
1. **Major publications in major journals** ← This is where your paper helps
2. Citations of your work by others
3. Original contributions of major significance
4. Judging the work of others
5. Membership in associations requiring outstanding achievement
6. Awards for excellence
7. Leading/critical role in distinguished organizations
8. High salary relative to others
9. Commercial success in arts
10. Recognition for achievements from experts

## JOURNAL STRATEGY FOR EB1A

### What Immigration Officers Look For

| Factor | Important | Very Important | Critical |
|--------|-----------|----------------|----------|
| Journal Impact Factor | >2.0 | >5.0 | >10.0 |
| Scopus Quartile | Q3+ | Q2+ | **Q1** |
| Publisher | MDPI, Springer | Elsevier, Wiley | **IEEE, ACM, Springer Nature** |
| Citation Potential | Moderate | High | **Very High** |
| Review Rigor | Standard | Thorough | **Highly selective** |

### RED FLAGS for EB1A (Avoid)

❌ MDPI journals (often viewed as "predatory" by immigration officers)
❌ Journals with >50% acceptance rate
❌ No impact factor or CiteScore
❌ Pay-to-publish without rigorous review
❌ Rapid publication (<30 days)

### GREEN FLAGS for EB1A (Target)

✅ Q1 journals with IF >3.0
✅ Traditional publishers (IEEE, ACM, Elsevier, Springer)
✅ Low acceptance rates (<30%)
✅ Rigorous peer review (2-4 months)
✅ High citation potential
