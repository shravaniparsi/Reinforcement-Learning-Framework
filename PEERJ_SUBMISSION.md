## PEERJ FORMATTING GUIDELINES

### Title

- Maximum 20 words
- No abbreviations
- Include key terms

### Abstract

- 200-300 words
- Include:
  - Background
  - Objective
  - Methods
  - Results
  - Conclusion
- No citations
- No abbreviations (unless defined)

### Keywords

- 5-8 keywords
- Include: Reinforcement Learning, Component Rendering, Web Performance, Next.js, Adaptive Optimization

### Sections

1. **Abstract** (200-300 words)
2. **Introduction** (~1500 words)
3. **Related Work** (~1500 words)
4. **Methodology** (~2000 words)
5. **Results** (~2000 words)
6. **Discussion** (~1500 words)
7. **Conclusion** (~500 words)
8. **References** (25 citations)
9. **Appendices** (if needed)

### Figures

- Minimum 300 DPI
- PDF preferred (vector graphics)
- PNG acceptable
- No figures in Word format
- Clear labels and legends
- Self-explanatory captions

### References

PeerJ uses author-year format:

```
Author, A. B., & Author, C. D. (2023). Title of article. Journal Name, Volume(Issue), Page-Page. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx
```

### Data Availability Statement

Required statement:

"All data and code are available at https://github.com/shravaniparsi/Reinforcement-Learning-Framework"
